## Findings allocated

Correct and add regression coverage for each observed failure:

1. `AssetLoader` must not treat absent or unknown `Content-Encoding` as identity when deriving numeric progress. Only a trustworthy identity representation with a validated positive length and matching EOF bytes may contribute to a determinate aggregate. Exposed chunked, contradictory, compressed, unknown, and mismatched responses remain indeterminate.
2. When a failed attempt is replaced by a retry, clear that attempt's byte count and trust before emitting the retry announcement. A parse failure after EOF must not announce the next attempt with the previous attempt's bytes.
3. Late-map adoption must preserve the source GLB map's flip convention, UV channel, wrap modes, and transform. Keep per-target texture state isolated when targets have different transforms.
4. Optional-map registration is atomic. If material assignment fails after temporary registration, restore visible material and all quality registries, release every derivative created by the failed transaction, and leave unrelated shared-material users unchanged.
5. A greeting transition timer is owned by its `WorldRuntime`; replacement greetings cancel the prior timer, stale timer callbacks cannot complete a later transition, and `dispose()` clears the timer synchronously.
6. The six regression cases must pass against the exact assembled R3+R4 source. Preserve R3's successful evidence and rerun relevant retained suites plus typecheck, lint, production build, and the source-bound browser paths that exercise these changes.
