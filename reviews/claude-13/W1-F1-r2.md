## 3. Findings Closure and Adjudication

### C13-01 (W1-01): Child Object World Transformations Applied Twice — CLOSED
- **Verification:** Inspection of `build-blockout.py` confirms that all parented objects now calculate and assign `matrix_parent_inverse = parent.matrix_world.inverted()` prior to setting parent relationships.
- **Evidence:** Native inspection log (`native-inspection.json`) and export inspection log (`export-inspection.json`) demonstrate 100% agreement across all 232 nodes. Door leaf closed center is verified at `(-1.2000, 1.0500, 1.8000)`, with hinge anchor at `(-1.6500, 0.0000, 1.8000)`. Pegboard, controllers, PC, headset, and microphone are verified in their authored physical coordinates.

### C13-02 (W1-02): Clearance Values Emitted as Hardcoded Literals — CLOSED
- **Verification:** Inspection of `build-blockout.py` confirms that clearances are now computed dynamically from evaluated mesh bounding box extrema and sampled rotation transforms (5° increments over 0°–90° door sweep).
- **Evidence:** Automated test suite `fault-injection.json` proves that mutating door wall proximity, armrest height, or chair base position results in immediate detected failure (`FAIL`). Real room blockout passes with 0.450m wall clearance, 1.670m desk clearance, 35mm armrest-to-tabletop gap, and 70mm chair base clearance.

## 4. Binding G1 Integration Requirements

Claude-13 reaffirms the following four contractual requirements as mandatory constraints for the downstream G1 combined proof loader:

1. **Identity Loading (C01-02 / C13-04):** Both W1 `room-blockout.glb` and W2 assets (`avatar-proof.glb`, `fixture-proof.glb`) carry pre-baked F1 spatial offsets. The runtime scene loader must add all roots strictly at identity `(0, 0, 0)` with no programmatic translation offset.
2. **Recursive Subtree Removal (C01-03 / C13-05):** Before attaching W2 avatar and chair assets, the loader must recursively dispose:
   - Node `resident` and all 17 child nodes.
   - Node `chair` and all 18 child nodes.
   - Locator `chair-root` must be replaced or reconciled with W2's articulated chair base.
3. **Fixture-Static Discard (C13-03):** Node `fixture-static` in `fixture-proof.glb` contains duplicate desk and floor proxy geometry and must be discarded upon load.
4. **Single Resident and Chair Invariant:** The final integrated scene graph must evaluate to exactly one resident entity and exactly one articulated chair entity.
