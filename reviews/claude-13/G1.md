## 3. Coordinate Systems, Placement & Double-Offset Invariants

### 3.1 Single Conversion Policy
- **Authoring Space:** Blender +Z up, +Y forward.
- **glTF Space:** Single conversion performed during Blender export `(x, y, z) -> (x, z, -y)` to produce +Y up, -Z forward.
- **Runtime Loader:** Three.js loader mounts all scenes strictly at identity:
  ```ts
  fixtureScene.position.set(0, 0, 0);
  avatarScene.position.set(0, 0, 0);
  ```
- **World Position Audit:**
  - W2 `avatar-proof.glb` root node `resident` possesses embedded local translation `[0.30, 0.00, -0.36]`.
  - W2 `fixture-proof.glb` root nodes `chair-root` and `chair-base` possess embedded local translation `[0.30, 0.00, -0.36]`.
  - Because scenes are mounted at `(0, 0, 0)`, the computed world coordinates evaluate to:
    - Resident Root: `(0.3000, 0.0000, -0.3600)`.
    - Chair Root: `(0.3000, 0.0000, -0.3600)`.
    - Chair Base: `(0.3000, 0.0000, -0.3600)`.
    - Desk Center: `(0.0000, 0.7500, -1.1500)`.
  - Deviation from canonical F1 coordinates: **< 0.0001m**.
  - Double offset (e.g. `(0.60, 0, -0.72)`): **PREVENTED / NOT PRESENT**.
