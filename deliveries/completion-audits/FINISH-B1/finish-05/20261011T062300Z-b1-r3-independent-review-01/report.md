## Confirmed findings

1. **Foot contact is not maintained during the chair turn and return.** The candidate report says the foot-floor gaps stay within floating-point tolerance ([candidate report, line 33](../../../../../../FINISH-B1-R3/report.md)). Fresh 60 Hz sampling of the exact delivered exports measures a 51.982 mm left-foot gap during `turn_to_visitor` at 0.233 s and a 51.995 mm right-foot gap at 0.400 s. During `return_to_work`, a foot gap also reaches 51.888 mm at 0.200 s. The pelvis-seat contact measurements remain close to zero, so the defect is specific to the foot-floor claim. See [browser-deformation-rerun.json](browser-deformation-rerun.json).

2. **The packaged resident/fixture builder does not reproduce the final exports.** The delivered resident is `bb0c6a…c2de2` and fixture is `0aeb5b…af6af30`. The maker's own path-guard receipt records a non-promoted resident build `d0d5d3…d97eec`; my isolated Blender 5.2.2 rerun produced resident `0ebfbb…3814b4` and fixture `0a138b…260dcb`. All have the expected file sizes, but none of the builder outputs match the delivered files. See [resident-builder-recheck.json](resident-builder-recheck.json) and the preserved [builder log](resident-builder-rerun.log). The final browser and import passes therefore prove the delivered bytes, while the packaged native source still does not recreate those bytes.

3. **Cancellation coverage is three cases, not the required 18.** The contract requires start, middle, and end cancellation for each of six finite clips. The rerun only cancels at the start of `notice_visitor`, middle of `turn_to_visitor`, and end of `return_to_work` within the greeting sequence. Its helper does not exercise `mouse_idle` or `attention_glance`. The three cases pass their local assertions, but the requirement remains FAIL. The maker report's “3/3 cancellation cases” is accurate for its limited harness; it is not full-contract coverage. See [browser-rerun-summary.json](browser-rerun-summary.json) and the source at `deliveries/FINISH-B1-R3/scripts/run-r3-verification.mjs:176-194`.

4. **Loop-seam continuity is not measured.** `coding_idle` and `breathing_idle` are specified as seamless loops. The browser suite samples their clips, but neither the maker nor reviewer output compares the start/end poses or velocities or records a seam assertion. This is NOT RUN, not a demonstrated loop pass.

5. **The production door owner is unresolved.** The standalone rerun reproduces the 2.5-second door sweep over 451 samples with no measured door/frame triangle intersections. It does not establish production behavior: no source in `app/src` writes `Door_Hinge`, and the candidate explicitly records the owner as not found. This remains a C2 integration gate. The geometry-only sweep is not a substitute for `EntranceCoordinator` ownership, interruption, skip, reduced-motion, and re-entry proof.

6. **Matched full-room visual comparisons and a deviation record are absent.** The 15 indexed browser views have 30/30 matching image hashes and dimensions; all eight native stills also match their hashes. However, `captures/comparisons/` is empty, the native index explicitly says its frames are only resident/fixture stills rather than full-room renders, and the view index has no deviation field. Nine supplemental PNGs are outside `captures/index.json` (although the output manifest hashes them). A direct look at the reference and desktop WebGL capture shows a substantially darker, sparser room, a mauve desk and dark chair where the reference has a bright ivory desk and blue/white chair, and simplified wall detail. The test HUD nevertheless calls the scene “Reference-faithful.” This is a reviewer observation, not user art acceptance; likeness, outfit, and visual acceptance remain NOT RUN.

7. **Both raw draw-call budgets still fail.** Fresh attribution matches intercepted render calls to `renderer.info`: desktop is 202 against 120; mobile is 146 against 80. The frozen assertion exits 1 with both failures. The candidate report labels these correctly. No target-tier runtime controller, C3 target mobile profile, cold-load test, or optional-overlap profile ran, so no future batching assumption can change the raw FAIL. See [draw-call-attribution-rerun.json](draw-call-attribution-rerun.json) and [draw-budget-assertion-rerun.log](draw-budget-assertion-rerun.log).

## Verified passes and limited evidence

Fresh `gltf-validator` checks pass all five exports with zero errors and zero warnings. Blender 5.2.2 imports all five exact final GLBs. Independent binary parsing confirms the door sibling/hinge/leaf hierarchy and transforms, separate keyboard/keycaps/mouse nodes, chair-root/chair-base and mesh-bearing `Chair_Seat`, the 26-joint resident skeleton, unique node names, all eight clip names/durations, and the reported mesh/triangle totals. The static-only batch evidence preserves required moving/contact targets and mesh bounds; raw budgets remain a separate fail.

The standalone browser run records 1,040 clip rows, 4,800 rows across 20 greeting/return repetitions, no resident root drift, zero desk-interior vertex hits, hand clearance of at least 159.995 mm during the turn, close pelvis-seat contact, and about 3.031 mm minimum mouse surface clearance. These are asset-harness results: the run does not mount C2's `CharacterDirector` or `EntranceCoordinator`. Browser console and page errors were empty. The 120-interval frame diagnostic per viewport is warm-cache/headless only.

The historical native failure receipt preserves the three original failure IDs and says their original frame arrays were never recorded. Its isolated replays do not reproduce all archived pair maps; do not interpret it as proof that the final delivered resident fixed those historical failures, since the tested native builder output and delivered export differ.
